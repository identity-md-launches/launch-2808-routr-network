The files in this directory are the unmodified ERC-20 dependency closure from
OpenZeppelin Contracts v5.1.0:
https://github.com/OpenZeppelin/openzeppelin-contracts/tree/v5.1.0

Included: ERC20.sol, IERC20.sol, IERC20Metadata.sol, Context.sol,
interfaces/draft-IERC6093.sol and the upstream MIT LICENSE.
They are ordinary source files, with no submodules or installation step.
